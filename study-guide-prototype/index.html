About these prototypes

The content is identical across all three approaches — only the way students reach it changes. These are for adult evaluation, not student use yet. Once a preferred approach is chosen, it will be ported to Google Apps Script for student deployment, since Vercel is not reachable on the school network.

Note: Sections 1–2 use existing Section 1 content. Sections 3–8 use standard biology content authored for this prototype and should be verified against the colleague's study guide before student use.
